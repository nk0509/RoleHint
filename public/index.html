The problems it solves

Most job seekers never find out why their CV didn't work. Roleproof gives you the feedback a recruiter would keep to themselves.

  • "I don't know if I'm good enough for this job."

    A fit score out of 100

    Scored on your skills, seniority, domain and measurable results, with a blunt verdict you can act on.

  • "I keep applying and hearing nothing back."

    Your gaps, ranked

    See what's holding you back, how serious each gap is, and a concrete way to fix it.

  • "My CV doesn't sound impressive."

    Rewrites of your own bullet points

    Stronger wording built from what you've done. Where a number is missing, you get a blank to fill in with the real figure.

  • "Software might be rejecting my CV."

    Missing keywords

    The terms this role expects that your CV doesn't mention yet.

  • "I don't know where to apply."

    Roles to target

    Five typical openings that suit your profile, each with a fit score and one-click job searches on LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri and more.

  • "What if I'm aiming at the wrong role?"

    Pivot roles

    Five nearby roles you could apply for now, with the headline and search terms to use for each.
