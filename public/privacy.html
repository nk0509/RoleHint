What we process

When you run an audit, the CV text, target role, location and any job description you enter are sent to our server and passed to Google, our AI provider, to create your report. Follow-up questions are sent the same way, along with the CV text and a summary of the report.

What we keep

We don't save CV text, reports or chat messages in a database, and our logs don't contain them. Your report exists only in your browser tab and is gone when you close it, unless you download it.

To enforce daily limits we keep a scrambled identifier made from your IP address, with a usage count, for up to two days. We also log token counts for each request, which can't identify you.

Who else receives data

  • Google generates the report through the paid Gemini API. Its handling of this data is set out in the Gemini API terms.
  • Vercel hosts the site and may log standard request details such as IP address.
  • Upstash stores the usage counters.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile runs the bot check, if it's turned on.

Files and browser storage

Uploaded PDF and Word files are read inside your browser. Only the extracted text is sent when you run an audit.

Your rights

You can ask us about, correct or delete personal data we hold, and withdraw consent, by writing to [CONTACT EMAIL]. Only upload a CV if it's yours or you have the person's permission. If you're in India, your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act apply. If you're in the EU or UK, your GDPR rights apply.
