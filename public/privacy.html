Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026. RoleHint is run by Nithish Kumar E, Karnataka, India. Contact: nithish.jitku@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Not stored. We don't save your CV, report or cover letter. Close the tab and they're gone unless you download or copy them.
  • Your choice. You agree before anything is sent. Your CV text goes to Google's AI to create the report.
  • Honest about limits. Reports and letters are AI drafts. Check everything, and only claim what's true.

What we process

When you run an audit, the CV text, target role, location and any job description you enter are sent to our server and passed to Google, our AI provider, to create your report. Follow-up questions are sent the same way, along with the CV text and a summary of the report. When you write a cover letter, the CV text, job title, company name, job description and tone you choose are sent the same way. When you run a certificate check, the CV text, job title and job description are sent the same way.

What we keep

We don't save CV text, reports, cover letters, certificate plans or chat messages in a database, and our logs don't contain them. Your report, letter or plan exists only in your browser tab and is gone when you close it, unless you download or copy it.

To enforce daily limits we keep a scrambled identifier made from your IP address, with a usage count, for up to two days. We also log token counts for each request, which can't identify you.

If you email us, your message and email address stay in our Gmail inbox, which Google runs, so we can reply. Please leave your CV out of the email.

Who else receives data

  • Google generates the report through the paid Gemini API. Its handling of this data is set out in the Gemini API terms.
  • Vercel hosts the site and may log standard request details such as IP address.
  • Upstash stores the usage counters.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile runs the bot check, if it's turned on.

Files and browser storage

Uploaded PDF and Word files are read inside your browser. Only the extracted text is sent when you run an audit, write a cover letter or run a certificate check. If you upload from the front page, or go from a report to a cover letter or certificate check, your browser holds the text for a moment while the next page opens, then removes it.

Your rights

You can ask us about, correct or delete personal data we hold, and withdraw consent, by writing to nithish.jitku@gmail.com. Only upload a CV if it's yours or you have the person's permission. If you're in India, your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act apply. If you're in the EU or UK, your GDPR rights apply.
